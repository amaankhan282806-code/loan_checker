YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

Privacy & data

Finwise stores your recent calculations in this browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them. Your personal details are not sent to a server unless you request AI guidance or enable Google Sheets integration.

When enabled, AI prompts are sent through a server-side function to Anthropic. For Sheets, records are sent to a configured Google Apps Script endpoint. Never enter sensitive identifiers or account credentials.
